How fills are simulated

Entry at the current candle close, with 0.02% adverse slippage and a 0.04% fee per fill. One position, maximum 1× notional. Stops and targets activate on the next candle. A stop takes priority if both are touched in one candle. Gaps can exceed planned loss. A target is a trigger-based exit in this model, not a guaranteed limit fill.

USD and USDT are treated as 1:1 paper account units. No borrow fees, funding, order-book liquidity or tax model. Short selling is simulated.

Import your own OHLCV CSV

Required: time, open, high, low, close. Volume is optional. Unix seconds, milliseconds or ISO dates are supported. Include a timezone offset for intraday data. Naive timestamps are parsed as UTC and displayed in New York. Date-only CSV values are normalized to that date at 4:00 p.m. New York. Minimum 10 candles; up to 10 MB.

CSV prices use paper dollars. Futures contract multipliers, FX pip values and other currencies are not converted.
