Fill assumptions

Current revealed close, plus 0.02% adverse slippage and 0.04% fee per fill. One position with at most 1× equity exposure. Stops and targets activate on the next candle; a stop wins if both are touched. Gaps can exceed planned loss.

All orders are simulated. Short availability, borrow costs, funding, order-book liquidity and taxes are not modeled.

Import your own OHLCV CSV

Required: time, open, high, low, close. Volume is optional. Unix seconds, milliseconds or ISO dates are supported. Include a timezone offset for intraday data. Naive timestamps are parsed as UTC and displayed in New York. Date-only CSV values are normalized to that date at 4:00 p.m. New York. Minimum 10 candles; up to 10 MB.

CSV prices use paper dollars. Futures contract multipliers, FX pip values and other currencies are not converted.
